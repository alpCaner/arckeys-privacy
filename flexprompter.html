FlexPrompter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17 (version 1.0)

FlexPrompter is a teleprompter and video recorder for Android, published by Alp Lab AB. This policy explains what happens to your scripts, your voice and your videos.

Summary

  • Alp Lab does not receive your data. FlexPrompter has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. Your scripts, takes and recordings stay on your phone unless you send them somewhere yourself.
  • Script sync (optional) copies your scripts to a hidden FlexPrompter folder in your own Google Drive, through Google's drive.appdata permission, so you can use them on another phone. The scripts go from your phone straight to Google, encrypted in transit; Alp Lab has no copy and cannot see them or your other Drive files. Because the scripts leave your phone, Google Play's Data safety section lists this as "Files and docs" collected for app functionality, optional. Turn sync off in Settings, and delete the copy in Drive → Settings → Manage apps → FlexPrompter → Delete hidden app data.
  • Live streaming (optional) sends your camera picture and sound to the platform you choose (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook or your own server) with your stream key. The key is stored encrypted on your phone only.
  • AI writing help (optional) runs entirely on your phone (Gemini Nano through Android AICore, on supported phones). The text you choose is not sent anywhere.
  • Voice follow hands microphone audio to your phone's on-device speech recognizer, a part of Android, while voice follow is listening. The recognised words only move the script and are not stored.
  • The framing guard looks for a face in the camera preview on your phone (Google ML Kit through Google Play services). No pictures leave the phone.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. FlexPrompter only stores a "Pro active" flag on your phone.

Cloud AI (larger AI models through an Alp Lab server) is not part of this version. If it is added later, this policy will describe it first, and the app will ask before anything is sent.

Permissions

CameraThe preview and the video of your takes. Only while the prompter screen is open.
MicrophoneThe sound of your takes, and voice follow. The microphone runs only during the countdown, while recording, and while voice follow is listening. Audio is never stored apart from the takes you record. With Pro you can pick an external USB, wired or Bluetooth microphone.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)Only when you use it: to let another phone with FlexPrompter work as a remote. The remote must type a code shown on your screen. FlexPrompter does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
Display over other appsOnly when you start the floating prompter: the script window stays on top of other apps (for example video calls). It shows text only and reads nothing from the apps underneath.
Notifications, foreground serviceThe floating prompter shows a notification with Pause/Play and Stop while it is on screen. With voice follow on, it uses the microphone while it listens, as described above.
Internet, network stateOnly for features you start: live streaming and script sync, as described above.

Sharing

Alp Lab does not sell or share your data, and FlexPrompter shares nothing by itself. Data goes to another company only through a feature you turn on: Google Drive for script sync, and the platform you pick for live streaming. When you tap Share on a take, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick.

Children

FlexPrompter is not directed at children under 13. Alp Lab does not receive personal data from anyone.
