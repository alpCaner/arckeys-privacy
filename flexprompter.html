FlexPrompter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16 (phone cameras only)

FlexPrompter is a teleprompter and video recorder for Android, published by Alp Lab AB. This policy explains what happens to your scripts, your voice and your videos.

Summary

  • FlexPrompter goes online only when you use an AI feature. Then the text you chose (a script, a selection, subtitles, or what you said in a take for feedback) is sent to the FlexPrompter AI server, which passes it through OpenRouter to an AI model (currently DeepSeek V4.1 Flash) and returns the result. Our server does not log or store it. Requests are sent only to model providers that, according to OpenRouter, do not keep the data or use it for training; OpenRouter itself processes the request under its own terms. Before the first AI use, the app asks you.
  • Simple AI edits may run entirely on your phone (Gemini Nano, Android AICore) instead.
  • To stop abuse, an AI request carries a Google Play Integrity token and, for Pro users, the Play purchase token, which our server checks with Google. Daily limits are counted with a one-way hash of an app install ID; it is not linked to you.
  • Your videos, audio recordings and scripts are never uploaded to us. We do not collect your data.
  • Assistant (optional, Pro): what you type, your creator profile (niche, audience, tone, platforms) and basic app context (filming mode, frame ratio) go through our server and OpenRouter to the AI model. When the assistant reads or changes a script, that script's text is sent too. Changes happen only after you tap to confirm. Our server keeps the conversation (with a one-way hash of your install ID) only while the chat is in use, and deletes all of it automatically 2 hours after the last message. It is not logged and not used for anything else. Your profile and chat history stay on your phone.
  • Script sync (optional) stores your scripts in a hidden FlexPrompter folder in your own Google Drive, through Google's drive.appdata permission. FlexPrompter has no server copy and cannot see your other Drive files. Delete the data in Drive → Settings → Manage apps → FlexPrompter → Delete hidden app data.
  • Live streaming (optional) sends your camera picture and sound to the platform you choose (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook or your own server) with your stream key. The key is stored encrypted on your phone only.
  • The framing guard looks for a face in the camera preview on your phone (Google ML Kit through Google Play services). No pictures leave the phone.
  • Voice follow hands microphone audio to your phone's on-device speech recognizer, a part of Android, while voice follow is listening.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. FlexPrompter only stores a "Pro active" flag on your phone.
  • No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no accounts.

Permissions

PermissionUsed for
CameraThe preview and the video of your takes. Only while the prompter screen is open.
MicrophoneThe sound of your takes, and voice follow. The microphone runs only during the countdown, while recording, and while voice follow is listening. Audio is never stored apart from the takes you record. With Pro you can pick an external USB, wired or Bluetooth microphone.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)Only when you use it: to let another phone with FlexPrompter work as a remote. The remote must type a code shown on your screen. FlexPrompter does not use Bluetooth to find your location.
Display over other appsOnly when you start the floating prompter: the script window stays on top of other apps (for example video calls). It shows text only and reads nothing from the apps underneath.
Notifications, foreground serviceThe floating prompter shows a notification with Pause and Stop while it is on screen.
Internet, network stateOnly for features you start: AI, live streaming and script sync, as described above.

Sharing

FlexPrompter shares nothing by itself. When you tap Share on a take, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick.

Children

FlexPrompter is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
