ArcKeys – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

ArcKeys is a keyboard (input method) for Android. This policy explains what happens to the text you type.

Summary

  • ArcKeys itself has no network access. The app does not request the internet permission, so ArcKeys cannot send what you type to any server, including ours. We do not collect your data.
  • Some optional features hand text or audio to Android system services that run on your phone (see below). They are part of Android / Google Play services, not of ArcKeys, and they are covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • If you choose to report an AI result, your email app sends that report to us. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.
  • The Pro purchase is processed by Google Play. ArcKeys only stores a "Pro owned" flag on your phone.
  • No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no accounts.

The AI model and voice language packs are downloaded and updated by Android, not by ArcKeys.

Reports you send us

The Report AI result button opens a report screen in ArcKeys that shows the text you gave the AI tool and the AI's answer, and asks for a reason. Send report then prepares an email in your own email app; you can still edit or cancel it there. If you send it, we use it only to look into problems with AI results, keep it no longer than 12 months, and delete it sooner if you ask us to at the address below.

When you send a sticker, the receiving app gets temporary read access to that one image only.

Backups (Pro). Back up writes one file to a place you choose (for example Downloads or your own cloud folder). It contains your settings, snippets, profiles, themes and, if you leave the option on, learned words. It never contains pinned clips or the Pro purchase. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. ArcKeys's own data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

ArcKeys never learns from password fields or from fields where the app asks for incognito / no personalised learning. It skips clipboard entries that Android marks as sensitive (passwords, one-time codes).

Android shows a standard warning when you enable any third-party keyboard, saying that it could collect what you type. ArcKeys does not collect it, and because the app has no internet permission it cannot send it anywhere itself.

Children

ArcKeys is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
